Privacy Policy

Inquest: Destinations has no advertising, developer-operated analytics, or third-party tracking. The developer does not operate an account system or gameplay backend.

Information stored on your device

On Apple devices, the app stores landmark attempts and completions, passport progress, route personal bests, and purchase-access state locally. Access-period markers in the Keychain can remain after the app is removed. On Android, app preferences hold attempted and solved cases, streaks, in-progress evidence, notes, settings, trial records and the cached unlock. Only a marker for access from a previous paid installation is eligible for protected Android backup or transfer. Gameplay, notes, settings, trial records and purchase caches are excluded. Optional Android reminders are scheduled on your device. The developer does not receive these records.

Purchases

Apple StoreKit or Google Play Billing handles the optional one-time unlock, including prices, purchases, restoration, and entitlement checks. Apple or Google processes payment under its own policies. The developer does not receive your complete payment-card details. The app maintains the local entitlement state required to provide access.

Game Center in earlier Apple editions

The current Apple app offers geography puzzles about real places and does not use Game Center. If you used Game Center in an earlier detective edition, that edition could send your daily solve time, rating and longest streak to Apple for its leaderboards when you signed in. Apple handles those leaderboard records under its own privacy policy.

Offline geography data

The app bundles its game content for offline play. On Apple devices, landmark content, map outlines and city coordinates are included with the app. The map and city data are from Natural Earth and are in the public domain. The app does not request your location or send your choices to a map service. Android may offer Google Play’s optional review prompt. The app sends no notes, case details or gameplay history with that request.

Support correspondence

If you email support, the developer receives the email address, message, and any attachments you choose to send. This information is used to answer your request and troubleshoot the problem. You can request deletion of support correspondence by contacting us.

Children

The developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children through the app. A parent or guardian can contact us about support correspondence sent by a child.

Changes and contact

This page will be updated when the app or its data practices change. Contact rizkcorsight@rizkcorsight.com with privacy questions.
