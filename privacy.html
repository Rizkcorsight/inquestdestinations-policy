Privacy Policy

Updated September 5, 2026 · iPhone and iPad

Privacy Policy

Inquest: Destinations has no advertising, developer-operated analytics, or third-party tracking. The developer does not operate an account system or gameplay backend.

Information stored on your device

The app stores landmark attempts and completions, passport progress, route personal bests, and purchase-access state locally. Access-period timestamps and an access-used marker are stored in the device Keychain and local preferences to enforce the 3-day access period. A Keychain record can remain after the app is removed. The app does not automatically send these records to the developer. Operating-system backup, transfer, and storage behavior follows your device and account settings.

Purchases

The app uses Apple StoreKit for its optional one-time full unlock. Loading product prices, purchasing, restoring, and checking entitlements use Apple services. Apple processes payment and related store information under its own policies. The developer does not receive your complete payment-card details. The app maintains the local entitlement state required to provide access.

Offline geography data

Landmark content, map outlines, and city coordinates are bundled with the app. The app does not request your location, send route choices to a map service, or require a network connection for geography puzzles during full access. Map and city data are from Natural Earth and are in the public domain.

Support correspondence

If you email support, the developer receives the email address, message, and any attachments you choose to send. This information is used to answer your request and troubleshoot the problem. You can request deletion of support correspondence by contacting us.

Children

The developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children through the app. A parent or guardian can contact us about support correspondence sent by a child.

Changes and contact

This page will be updated when the app or its data practices change. Contact rizkcorsight@rizkcorsight.com with privacy questions.
