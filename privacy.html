INQUEST: DESTINATIONS
privacy policy

Effective August 5, 2026

Privacy Policy

Inquest: Destinations does not include advertising, developer-operated analytics, or tracking. The developer does not operate an account system or backend server for the app. The app does use Apple and Google platform services for purchases, ratings, and certain optional features, as described below.

Information kept by the app

Inquest: Destinations saves information needed to run the game and remember your choices. This includes settings and language preferences; notification choices; detective rating and streak information; attempted and solved case history; best results; in-progress case notes and selections; access-window and purchase-entitlement state; widget state; and aggregate gameplay counters used to make the experience work.

This app data is stored locally on your device. The app does not automatically send those gameplay records or counters to the developer. On Android, normal cloud backup is restricted to an install-history marker used to preserve eligible paid-download access; gameplay, settings, notes, purchase caches, and trial clocks are excluded from that backup configuration. Your operating system may otherwise handle device storage or transfer according to your device and account settings.

Purchases and entitlement checks

The app offers a one-time full-archive unlock. On Android it uses Google Play Billing 9.1.0. On iOS and Mac Catalyst it uses Apple StoreKit. When the app loads product information, makes a purchase, restores a purchase, or verifies an entitlement, it communicates with the applicable store through that platform's APIs.

Apple or Google processes the purchase and related store information under its own privacy policy. The developer does not receive your complete payment-card details. The app keeps only the local purchase and entitlement state needed to provide access and reconcile it with the store.

Ratings and reviews

On Android, the app includes Google Play In-App Review 2.0.2. On Apple platforms, it uses Apple's StoreKit review prompt. These system-managed interfaces may contact the applicable app store so that it can decide whether to display a review prompt and, if you choose, accept a rating or review. The developer does not operate that service and does not use it to transmit your case history, notes, or local gameplay counters.

Game Center on Apple platforms

Inquest: Destinations supports Apple Game Center. If you authenticate with Game Center, the app can submit a daily solve score derived from solve time, your detective rating, and your longest streak to the app's leaderboards. Apple handles Game Center identity, authentication, leaderboards, and network traffic under Apple's terms and privacy policy. Game Center is not used on Android.

Network access

The Android build includes network-state and internet access through its packaged platform-service dependencies. The Apple build has network access for StoreKit and Game Center. Core case files and localized game content are bundled with the app, but some functions—including loading store product information, purchasing, restoring or verifying access, opening store-managed review prompts, and using Game Center—depend on a network connection and the relevant platform service.

Notifications and widgets

If you enable reminders, the app schedules notifications on your device for daily cases, streak protection, or access-window timing. Android requests notification permission on supported versions and listens for reboot and time changes so opted-in reminders can be rebuilt. The home-screen widget uses a limited local snapshot of game state. The developer does not receive notification delivery history or widget data.

Support email and information you choose to send

If you contact support, the developer receives the email address, message, and attachments or diagnostic text that you choose to send. The app can prepare a non-identifying summary of on-device aggregate counters in a mail composer; you can inspect, edit, or delete it, and nothing is sent unless you choose to send the email. Support correspondence is used to answer your request and troubleshoot the issue.

No advertising, tracking, or developer analytics

The app does not contain advertising code, cross-app tracking, or a developer analytics service. It does not sell personal information. Local aggregate counters are not transmitted automatically and are not connected to an advertising identifier or a developer account.

Children

The game contains fictional crime and murder-mystery text and is not directed to young children. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children through the app. If a child contacts support, a parent or guardian may ask for the correspondence to be deleted.

Changes to this policy

If the app or its data practices change, this page will be updated and the effective date above will be revised. Material changes will be reflected before they apply to a released version when practicable.
