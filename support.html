Help with the destination files

Support

For help with Inquest: Destinations, email rizkcorsight@rizkcorsight.com. Include your platform (iPhone, iPad, Mac, or Android), app version, and a short description of what happened. If the issue concerns a case, include the case number but avoid sending spoilers in the subject line.

This support page is specifically for Inquest: Destinations (bundle ID com.rizkcorsight.inquestdestinations, version 1.0.0), not the original Inquest app.

A purchase or restore is not completing

Confirm that the device is online and signed in to the same Apple App Store or Google Play account used for the purchase. Reopen the app, then use the restore or purchase control again. Store product details, purchases, entitlement checks, and restores are provided by Apple StoreKit or Google Play Billing, so they may be unavailable during a store outage or account restriction.

If access is still missing, do not purchase repeatedly. Email support with the platform, app version, the approximate purchase date, and any message shown by the app. Do not send payment-card details or account passwords.

How does the daily case work?

The app selects the case assigned to the current date and provides an archive of destination mysteries. Case content ships inside the app. Your attempted cases, solved cases, rating, streak, best results, and unfinished case drafts are saved by the app so that play can continue across launches.

I left a case unfinished

An unattempted case keeps its in-progress draft, including the evidence and interview choices already made. Return to that case from the app to continue. Once a case is resolved, replay behavior may differ from the first rated attempt.

Notifications are not appearing

Open the app's Settings and verify that the reminder you want is enabled. Then check system notification permission for Inquest: Destinations. Android 13 and later require notification permission; Apple platforms also require the user to approve notifications. Battery-saving or focus modes can delay or suppress alerts. Reminders are scheduled on the device, so changing the timezone or device clock can cause the schedule to be rebuilt.

Game Center or leaderboards are unavailable

Game Center is an Apple-platform feature. Check that you are signed in to Game Center in system Settings and that the device is online, then relaunch the app. Inquest: Destinations submits its own daily-solve, detective-rating, and longest-streak scores. Android does not use Game Center.

The rating prompt did not appear

Apple and Google control whether their in-app review interface is shown. The app can request the prompt at an appropriate moment, but the platform may decide not to display it. This is expected and does not affect gameplay or purchases.

The widget is not updating

Open Inquest: Destinations once so it can refresh its local widget snapshot, then return to the home screen. If needed, remove and re-add the widget. On Android, confirm the app has not been force-stopped. Widget information is kept locally and reflects a limited summary rather than the complete case state.

How do I report a typo or an unfair clue?

Email the case number, language selected in the app, and the exact sentence or clue that needs review. A screenshot is helpful when it does not reveal unrelated private notifications or device information. Reports are reviewed for a future app update.

What information should I include in a support request?

The most useful details are your device type, operating-system version, app version, selected language, the screen you were using, and steps that reproduce the problem. The app may offer to place an on-device aggregate diagnostic summary into your email. You can read and edit that text before sending it. Nothing is sent to the developer until you press Send.

Privacy

Inquest: Destinations has no ads, developer-operated analytics, or tracking. It does use Apple and Google services for purchases and review prompts, and optional Game Center on Apple platforms. Read the Privacy Policy for the complete explanation.
