Support

For help with Inquest: Destinations, email rizkcorsight@rizkcorsight.com. Include your device, operating-system version, app version, language, and the steps that led to the problem.

How does the Atlas challenge work?

Keep the starting city fixed and use the arrow controls to reorder the other stops. Choose 3 to 6 stops. Check your route to compare its total distance with the shortest possible order. You can inspect each leg, improve your route, or reveal the shortest route. Revealing the solution ends scoring for that attempt. The daily challenge changes at midnight UTC; practice routes can be started at any time.

What do the distances mean?

Challenges use 53 city centers from Natural Earth. Distances are approximate great-circle distances between city centers. They are geography puzzles, not road, flight, or travel directions. The map and city data are bundled for offline play.

How do landmark puzzles and passport stamps work?

Open the catalog to explore 73 real-world landmarks in 48 countries. Use the original stylized illustration and city clue to identify the landmark and country. A correct answer records a regional passport stamp. The illustrations are artistic interpretations.

Full access and restoring a purchase

Start with 3 days of full access at no charge. When that period ends, an optional one-time purchase permanently unlocks the catalog and route challenges. There is no subscription and the access period does not automatically charge you. The purchase button shows the price supplied by the App Store for your region.

To restore an existing purchase, connect to the internet, use the same Apple Account that made the purchase, and choose Restore Purchase. If a purchase is pending or an error appears, wait for the store to resolve it. Do not buy repeatedly; contact support with the message shown. Never send passwords or payment-card details.

Progress and privacy

Passport progress, route personal bests, and access state are stored on your device. Gameplay works offline during full access. Loading prices, purchasing, and restoring require Apple services and a network connection. Read the Privacy Policy for details.

Report a geographic or language error

Email the landmark or city, the route number if shown, the language, and the text or screenshot that needs correction. We use reports to improve future updates.
